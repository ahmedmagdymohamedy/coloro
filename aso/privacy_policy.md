# Privacy Policy — Coloro

_Last updated: 1 September 2026_

Coloro ("the game") is published by **Megz**. This policy explains what the
game collects and why. Host this file at a public URL (GitHub Pages, your
own site, or a free policy host) and paste that URL into Play Console and
App Store Connect — both stores refuse to publish an ad-supported app
without one.

> **This text must stay in step with the Play "Data safety" form.** Google
> rejects an update when the two disagree, and it re-scans the app's actual
> network traffic on every release. The declared set as of 1 Sep 2026 is:
> approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or other
> IDs — all **collected and shared**, for advertising, analytics, fraud
> prevention, and app functionality. Any new SDK changes this list.

## What the game stores on your device

Your level progress and sound setting are saved **locally on your device
only**. They are never uploaded, and deleting the app deletes them.

The game has no accounts, no login, no chat, and no user-generated content.

## What third parties collect

Coloro shows ads and measures its own performance. To do that it sends a
small amount of data off your device to the companies below. In every case
the data is sent over an encrypted (TLS) connection, and none of it includes
your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Across all of them the data amounts to: your device's **advertising
identifier and similar device identifiers**, an **approximate location**
worked out from your IP address (country/city level, never a precise
position), **which parts of the game and which ads you interacted with**,
and **technical diagnostics** such as load times and errors.

**Google AdMob** — serves the banner, interstitial and rewarded ads that keep
the game free, and is also the mediator that decides which ad network fills
each slot. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Unity Ads** and **AppLovin** — additional ad networks that compete for
those same ad slots through AdMob mediation (Android only). When one of them
wins, it receives the advertising identifier and ad-request data it needs to
choose and measure the ad.
See https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy and
https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

**Firebase Analytics** — records anonymous gameplay events (a level was
started, completed or lost, and an ad was shown) so we can see which levels
are too hard and fix them. It collects an app-instance identifier and basic
device information.
See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

**Meta (Facebook) App Events** — reports app installs and a few gameplay
milestones so we can tell which of our ads actually bring in players.
Android only. See https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy

**Firebase Cloud Messaging** — only if you allow notifications. It stores a
device registration token so we can send occasional announcements about the
game. The token identifies the app installation, not you.

## Notifications

Coloro asks permission to send notifications after you finish level 3. You
can decline, and the game plays exactly the same.

If you allow them, the game schedules **reminders on your device**: one a day
after you last played, and one a week after you last played. Opening the game
resets both, so you never receive a reminder while you are still playing.
These are generated on your phone and are not sent from a server.

You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings, and the
scheduled reminders stop with them.

## Your choices

- **Reset your advertising ID** or opt out of personalised ads:
  Android → Settings → Google → Ads.
  iOS → Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Delete everything**: uninstalling the game removes all local data.

## Children

Coloro is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided
personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
